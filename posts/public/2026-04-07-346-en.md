# LinkedIn Post — 2026-04-07 (EN) — Tribute

---

Lion Air Flight 610. 29 October 2018.

189 people died. A flight from Jakarta to Pangkal Pinang. Thirteen minutes after takeoff.

178 adults. One child, two infants, six cabin crew, two pilots.

Indonesian government officials, Ministry of Finance employees, Audit Board inspectors, a captain from India, a former professional cyclist from Italy.

They never landed.

---

Ethiopian Airlines Flight 302. 10 March 2019.

157 people died. A flight from Addis Ababa to Nairobi. Six minutes after takeoff.

35 nationalities: 32 Kenyans, 18 Canadians, 9 Ethiopians. Aid workers, UN staff. Human rights defenders. Public health workers. A 36-year-old from Devon on her way to a UN environment conference. A Slovak politician, his wife, and their two children. A Nigerian-Canadian academic. A Ugandan peacekeeper. An Irish World Food Programme worker — his youngest child was seven months old.

They were going to Nairobi to make the world better. They never arrived.

---

346 people total. Rest in Peace.

The criminal case against Boeing was dismissed on 6 November 2025.
