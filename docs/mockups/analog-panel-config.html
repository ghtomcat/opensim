Analog panel — flowing layout rules

The data gives each aircraft's instruments in groups and in order (the photo's row breaks as a hint). The layout fits them to the view: the panel view (the full screen above the status bar) or the combined view (a strip under the outside view). Panel view: the photo's rows always. Combined view: every way to break the groups into rows, side by side or stacked, is scaled to fit; the largest dials win — the photo's own rows if they come within 85 % of the best. A large dial is never bigger than 200 px; the panel is centred. Sizes are size classes, their ratios kept. Simulated screen: 1440 × 900.

Size classes: L 80 mm · S 57 mm · XS 45 mm · T 25 mm (lamps) — relative sizes; data and sources as in the plan (docs/issues.md). Kinds: dial (one needle), twin (two needles on one face), glass (sight glass), lamp (warning light), clock, level (curved bubble inclinometer). Layout: a board (one panel, rows centred) or flight + engine blocks.
